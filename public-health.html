Public Health & Health Promotion

Disease prevention, surveillance, outbreak response, environmental health and community health programmes across Morobe Province.

Public health is a core function of the Morobe Provincial Health Authority. Our teams work to prevent disease, promote healthy behaviours, protect communities from environmental risks, and respond quickly when outbreaks or health emergencies occur.

Public health programmes operate in partnership with the National Department of Health, district teams, churches, community leaders and development partners to reach people in urban, rural and remote settings.

🎯 Priority Focus Areas

Public health efforts are guided by national priorities and the specific needs of Morobe communities.

Communicable Disease Control TB, HIV, malaria, vaccine-preventable diseases and emerging threats.
Maternal & Child Health Supporting safe pregnancy, delivery, newborn care and child survival.
Nutrition Addressing undernutrition and promoting healthy diets, especially for mothers and children.
Water, Sanitation & Hygiene Improving access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene practices.
Health Education Raising awareness on prevention, early care-seeking and healthy lifestyles.
Emergency Preparedness Planning and capacity for rapid response to outbreaks and disasters.

🤝 Working with Communities

Public health succeeds when communities, facilities and partners work together.

Communities can support public health by:

  • Following official health advice and vaccination schedules
  • Reporting unusual illness clusters to the nearest health facility
  • Practising good hygiene, safe water use and proper sanitation
  • Participating in health promotion activities and campaigns
  • Supporting community health workers and village health volunteers

For the latest official guidance, always check Health Alerts and notices published by MPHA.
