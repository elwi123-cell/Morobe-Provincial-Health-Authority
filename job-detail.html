Clinical Nurse (Registered)

📍 ANGAU Memorial Provincial Hospital, Lae · Full-time · Reference: MPHA-CN-2025-08
Open
Closing date: 31 October 2025 Category: Nursing & Clinical

Position Overview

The Morobe Provincial Health Authority is seeking a qualified and motivated Registered Clinical Nurse to join the team at ANGAU Memorial Provincial Hospital. The successful candidate will deliver high-quality patient care, support clinical programmes and contribute to continuous improvement of nursing services across the hospital.

This role is ideal for an experienced nurse who is committed to serving the people of Morobe Province and who thrives in a busy hospital environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Provide direct clinical nursing care to patients in assigned wards or units in accordance with professional standards and hospital protocols.
  • Assess, plan, implement and evaluate individualised patient care plans in collaboration with the multidisciplinary team.
  • Administer medications and treatments safely and accurately, and maintain clear clinical documentation.
  • Support infection prevention and control practices and contribute to a safe clinical environment.
  • Participate in health education, discharge planning and patient/family counselling as required.
  • Assist with mentoring and supervision of student nurses and junior staff when directed.
  • Contribute to quality improvement, clinical audits and reporting activities.
  • Perform other duties as reasonably assigned by the Nursing Manager or Clinical Supervisor.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Current registration as a Registered Nurse with the Nursing Council of Papua New Guinea (or eligible for registration).
  • Diploma or Bachelor of Nursing from a recognised institution.
  • Minimum of two (2) years post-registration clinical experience in a hospital or health facility setting (preferred).
  • Strong clinical assessment, communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to work shifts, including nights, weekends and public holidays as rostered.
  • Commitment to patient-centred care, professional ethics and continuous learning.
  • PNG citizenship or valid work permit; local applicants from Morobe Province are encouraged to apply.

What We Offer

  • Competitive public sector salary and conditions in line with the Public Service General Orders.
  • Opportunity to work at the province’s main referral hospital and contribute to improved health outcomes.
  • Professional development and training support where available.
  • A supportive multidisciplinary team environment.

How to Apply

Interested applicants should submit the following documents:

  • Cover letter addressing the key selection criteria
  • Updated curriculum vitae (CV)
  • Certified copies of relevant qualifications and nursing registration
  • Contact details of three (3) professional referees

Applications should be addressed to the Human Resources Manager, Morobe Provincial Health Authority, and submitted by the closing date.

Important: Late applications will not be considered. Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted. MPHA is an equal opportunity employer and encourages applications from qualified women and persons with disability.
