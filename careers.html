Careers & Vacancies

Join the Morobe Provincial Health Authority and help improve health outcomes for communities across Morobe Province.

The Morobe Provincial Health Authority employs doctors, nurses, midwives, community health workers, allied health professionals, administrators and support staff who work together to deliver health services across the province.

We are committed to fair, transparent recruitment in line with approved procedures. Only vacancies formally approved and advertised by the Authority should be treated as current opportunities.

How to Apply

General guidance. Always follow the specific instructions in each vacancy notice.

1

Check the vacancy

Read the full advertisement, eligibility criteria and closing date.

2

Prepare documents

Gather required certificates, CV and any forms specified in the notice.

3

Submit application

Apply by the stated method and deadline. Late applications are usually not accepted.

Online applications, if offered, require secure form processing. Do not send clinical or sensitive personal information through unsecured channels. For formal recruitment queries, contact the Authority’s Human Resources or corporate office.
