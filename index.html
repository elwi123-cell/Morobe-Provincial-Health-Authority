Our Key Services

MPHA delivers a wide range of clinical and public health services to communities across Morobe Province.

👶 Maternal & Child Health

Antenatal care, safe delivery services, immunisation, nutrition and child health programmes.

🛡️ Public Health & Prevention

Disease surveillance, outbreak response, health promotion and environmental health services.

🏥 Hospital & Clinical Care

Inpatient and outpatient services, emergency care, surgery and specialised clinical support.

💊 Pharmacy & Medical Supplies

Essential medicines, medical supplies coordination and pharmaceutical services.

🧠 Mental Health & Wellbeing

Community mental health support, counselling and psychosocial care services.

📋 Health Information & Planning

Health data, strategic planning, monitoring and evaluation for better service delivery.
