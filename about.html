About the Authority

Learn about the Morobe Provincial Health Authority, our mandate, vision and commitment to improving health outcomes across Morobe Province.

🎯

Our Vision

A healthy and resilient Morobe Province where every person has access to quality, equitable and people-centred health services.

🧭

Our Mission

To deliver, coordinate and continuously improve public health and clinical services that save lives and strengthen communities across Morobe.

💚

Our Purpose

Improving Health. Saving Lives. Serving Morobe. — guiding every decision, partnership and service we provide.

Who We Are

The Morobe Provincial Health Authority (MPHA) is the statutory body responsible for the planning, management and delivery of public health and clinical services throughout Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea.

We work in close partnership with the National Department of Health, the Morobe Provincial Government, development partners, churches and communities to ensure that health services reach people where they live — from urban centres to remote rural and island communities.

Our Mandate

MPHA is mandated to:

  • Provide and manage hospital, primary health care and public health services across the province
  • Coordinate disease prevention, surveillance and outbreak response
  • Support maternal, newborn, child and adolescent health programmes
  • Ensure the availability of essential medicines and medical supplies
  • Plan, monitor and report on health service performance and health outcomes
  • Engage communities and partners in improving health and wellbeing

Our Core Values

These values guide how we work every day:

Integrity We act honestly, transparently and accountably in all we do.
Respect We treat every person with dignity, cultural sensitivity and fairness.
Excellence We strive for continuous improvement and high-quality care.
Compassion We serve with empathy and put patients and communities first.
Teamwork We collaborate across facilities, partners and sectors.
Equity We work to close health gaps and reach those most in need.

Our Service Network

MPHA oversees a network of health facilities that includes provincial and district hospitals, health centres, sub-health centres and aid posts. ANGAU Memorial Provincial Hospital is a central facility within this network, providing specialised referral and tertiary-level care for the province and surrounding areas.

Our teams of doctors, nurses, community health workers, allied health professionals and support staff work together to deliver both preventive and curative services.

👥

Leadership & Governance

MPHA is governed by a Board and led by an executive management team responsible for strategic direction, performance and accountability.
