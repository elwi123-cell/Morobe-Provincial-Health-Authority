Partners & Stakeholders

Working together with government, churches, development partners and communities to improve health across Morobe Province.

The Morobe Provincial Health Authority does not work alone. Effective health services depend on strong partnerships with the National Department of Health, the Morobe Provincial Government, church health services, development partners, communities and other stakeholders.

We value collaboration that is aligned with national and provincial health priorities, transparent and focused on sustainable results for the people of Morobe.

🤝 Working With MPHA

Guidance for organisations interested in formal partnership or collaboration.

Engagement principles

Partnerships should align with the Authority’s strategic priorities and national health policy. Formal collaboration typically involves:

  • Clear agreement on objectives, roles and responsibilities
  • Alignment with approved provincial and national plans
  • Transparency in reporting and use of resources
  • Respect for local systems, staff and communities
  • Sustainable approaches that strengthen, rather than bypass, existing services

Organisations wishing to discuss partnership opportunities should contact the Authority through official channels. Unsolicited proposals may be directed to the corporate or planning office as appropriate.
