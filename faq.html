Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about the Morobe Provincial Health Authority, services, facilities and how to get help.

Find quick answers below. If you need clinical care or urgent help, contact your nearest health facility or use the Emergency Contacts page. For other enquiries, see Contact Us.

About MPHA

The Morobe Provincial Health Authority (MPHA) is the official body responsible for the delivery and management of public health services across Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea. It oversees hospitals, health centres, clinics and public health programmes in the province.

Learn more on the About page.

ANGAU Memorial Provincial Hospital is a major referral hospital within the Morobe Provincial Health Authority’s network. It provides specialised and secondary-level care and supports referral pathways from other facilities in the province.

See the Facilities Directory for more information.

Services & Facilities

Use the Facilities Directory to browse hospitals, health centres and clinics in Morobe Province. Facility detail pages list services, contacts and other practical information where available.

Services include clinical care at hospitals and health centres, maternal and child health, public health programmes (surveillance, immunisation, health promotion), and other priority programmes. Availability varies by facility.

See Services, Maternal & Child Health and Public Health.

This depends on the facility and service. Many outpatient and clinic services operate on walk-in or scheduled clinic days. For specialised services, contact the facility directly to confirm requirements.

Emergencies & Urgent Care

Go to the nearest hospital or health centre immediately, or call official emergency numbers. Do not rely solely on website contact forms for urgent care.

See the Emergency Contacts page for official numbers and guidance.

Careers, Feedback & Other

Approved vacancies are advertised on the Careers page and through official channels. Always follow the application instructions and closing dates in each notice. Contact HR or the corporate office for formal recruitment queries.

You can give feedback at the facility where you received care, by phone or in writing to the Authority, or through formal complaints channels. See the Feedback & Complaints page for guidance.

Use the Contact Us page for general and administrative enquiries. For facility-specific services, contact the facility directly. For emergencies, use emergency contacts and go to the nearest facility.
