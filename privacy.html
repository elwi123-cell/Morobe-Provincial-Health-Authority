Privacy Policy

How the Morobe Provincial Health Authority collects, uses, stores and protects personal and health information.

The Morobe Provincial Health Authority (MPHA) is committed to protecting the privacy of individuals whose personal and health information we hold. This page outlines general principles for how information is handled in connection with our website and services.

This is a general privacy notice for public information purposes. Specific clinical, employment and legal obligations are governed by applicable laws and internal policies of the Authority.

Last updated: 2025 · For formal privacy enquiries, contact the Authority via the Contact page.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect or hold:

  • Contact details you provide (e.g. name, phone, email) when you enquire or give feedback
  • Information related to job applications if you apply for vacancies
  • Website usage information (e.g. pages visited) if analytics are enabled
  • Health and personal information collected in the course of providing clinical care (handled under clinical and legal frameworks)

We only collect information that is reasonably necessary for our functions and activities.

2. How We Use Information

Personal information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and feedback
  • Manage recruitment and employment processes
  • Deliver and improve health services
  • Meet legal, reporting and accountability obligations
  • Communicate official notices and health information where appropriate

Health information collected for care is used for treatment, continuity of care and related lawful purposes.

3. Disclosure of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared where required or permitted by law, including:

  • With other health services for continuity of care
  • With government agencies where legally required
  • With service providers who support our operations under appropriate agreements
  • Where you have consented, or where disclosure is otherwise authorised

4. Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal and health information from misuse, loss, unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Security measures include physical, administrative and technical controls appropriate to the sensitivity of the information.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We encourage users to take care when sending sensitive information online.

5. This Website

This website may collect limited technical information (such as IP address or browser type) for security and operational purposes. If cookies or analytics tools are used in future, this notice will be updated to describe them.

Links to external websites (e.g. National Department of Health) are provided for convenience. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

6. Access and Correction

You may request access to personal information we hold about you, or request correction of inaccurate information, subject to legal and operational requirements. Requests should be made through official channels via the Contact page.

Access to clinical records is managed according to health facility and legal procedures.

7. Changes to This Notice

This privacy notice may be updated from time to time. The version published on this website is the current public version. Significant changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date or by additional notice where appropriate.

8. Contact About Privacy

For privacy-related enquiries, requests or concerns, please contact the Morobe Provincial Health Authority through the channels listed on the Contact Us page. For clinical record requests, contact the relevant facility.

ℹ️

Official Information Notice — This privacy notice is a general public summary. It does not replace applicable laws, clinical confidentiality rules or internal policies of the Morobe Provincial Health Authority. Formal privacy or data protection policies may be issued separately by the Authority.
