ANGAU Memorial Provincial Hospital

Provincial Referral Hospital · Lae, Morobe Province

Referral Hospital Emergency Services Surgery Maternity Outpatient

About this Facility

ANGAU Memorial Provincial Hospital is the principal referral hospital within the Morobe Provincial Health Authority network. It provides specialised clinical services, emergency care, surgery, medical and maternity services for patients from across Morobe Province and surrounding areas.

The hospital plays a central role in the provincial health system, receiving referrals from district hospitals, health centres and lower-level facilities, and supporting training and clinical leadership.

Practical Information

Visiting & Access

Patients and visitors should follow hospital visiting hours and any current infection-control or security procedures. For non-emergency care, contact the hospital or your referring facility in advance where possible.

Referrals

Referrals to ANGAU Memorial Provincial Hospital are normally made by lower-level health facilities according to clinical need and established referral pathways. Emergency cases are assessed on arrival.
