Governance & Policies

How the Morobe Provincial Health Authority is governed, the policies that guide our work, and our commitment to accountability and transparency.

Governance Overview

The Morobe Provincial Health Authority operates within the legal and policy framework of Papua New Guinea. Governance is provided by a Board that sets strategic direction and oversees performance, while the Chief Executive Officer and executive team manage day-to-day operations and service delivery.

We are accountable to the people of Morobe Province, the National Department of Health, and the Morobe Provincial Government. Our governance arrangements aim to ensure that health services are effective, equitable, transparent and responsive to community needs.

Legislative & Policy Framework

MPHA functions under relevant national and provincial legislation and aligns its work with national health policies and plans. Key elements of the framework include:

📜 National Legislation

Relevant Acts of Parliament governing health services, public authorities and public finance in Papua New Guinea.

🏛️ National Health Policy

Alignment with National Department of Health policies, standards and strategic directions.

🗺️ Provincial Priorities

Coordination with Morobe Provincial Government development and health priorities.

📋 Internal Policies

Board-approved policies covering clinical governance, finance, human resources and operations.

Board Governance

The Board of the Morobe Provincial Health Authority provides strategic leadership and oversight. Its core responsibilities include:

  • Setting and reviewing the Authority’s strategic plan and priorities
  • Approving major policies, budgets and performance targets
  • Monitoring organisational and clinical performance
  • Ensuring compliance with legal, financial and ethical requirements
  • Appointing and supporting the Chief Executive Officer
  • Promoting transparency, risk management and continuous improvement

Board members bring experience from health, public administration, community and other relevant sectors. The Board operates through regular meetings and may establish committees to support focused oversight in areas such as audit, finance and clinical quality.

Key Policy Areas

The Authority maintains policies and procedures that guide staff and services. Priority policy domains include:

🩺

Clinical Governance & Quality

Standards for safe, effective and patient-centred care, incident reporting and continuous quality improvement.

💰

Financial Management

Budgeting, procurement, asset management and compliance with public finance rules.

👥

Human Resources

Recruitment, workforce development, codes of conduct, performance management and staff welfare.

🔒

Privacy & Information

Protection of patient information, records management and responsible use of health data.

📢

Public Communication

Official announcements, health alerts, media engagement and public feedback processes.

🤝

Partnerships & Procurement

Engagement with partners, transparent tendering and ethical management of contracts and resources.

Accountability & Reporting

MPHA is committed to transparent reporting on its performance, use of resources and progress against strategic goals. Accountability mechanisms typically include:

1

Strategic Planning

Clear goals and indicators set in the Authority’s strategic and annual plans.

2

Performance Monitoring

Regular review of service delivery, clinical quality and financial performance.

3

Public Reporting

Annual reports, audited statements and key publications made available to stakeholders.

Documents & Publications

Approved strategic plans, annual reports, policies and public notices are published through official channels when authorised.
