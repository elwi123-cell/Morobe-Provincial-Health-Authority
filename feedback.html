Feedback & Complaints

Share feedback, raise concerns or make a formal complaint about health services provided by the Morobe Provincial Health Authority.

Your feedback helps the Morobe Provincial Health Authority improve services. We welcome compliments, suggestions and formal complaints about care and services delivered through the provincial health network.

Please use the channels below for non-emergency feedback. For medical emergencies, go to the nearest health facility or use official emergency contacts.

💬 Ways to Give Feedback

Choose the option that best fits your situation.

🏥

At the Facility

Speak with the officer in charge, patient liaison or nurse manager at the hospital or health centre where you received care.

🔄 How Complaints Are Handled

A general overview of a typical formal complaints process.

1

Receive

Your complaint is recorded and acknowledged where possible

2

Review

The matter is assessed and referred to the appropriate area

3

Respond

You may receive a response or update according to local procedures

Timelines and procedures may vary. Serious clinical or safety concerns may be escalated more quickly. Always provide accurate details and your preferred contact method so the Authority can follow up if needed.

💡 Tips for Effective Feedback

Clear information helps the Authority respond more effectively.

Include where possible

  • Date, time and location (facility or service) of the event
  • Names of staff involved if known and relevant
  • A clear description of what happened and what outcome you seek
  • Your name and contact details if you want a response
  • Any supporting documents (e.g. discharge notes) if relevant

📝 Online Feedback Form

Secure form processing can be enabled when backend systems are available.

An online feedback form requires secure submission and handling of personal information. Until that is configured, please use facility-level feedback, phone or written channels through the Authority’s official contact points.
