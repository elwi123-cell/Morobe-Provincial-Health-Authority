Events & Campaigns

Health campaigns, immunisation drives, community activities and official events supported by the Morobe Provincial Health Authority.

MPHA supports and coordinates health events and campaigns that reach communities across Morobe Province. These include immunisation campaigns, health promotion days, community outreach and other official activities.

Dates and locations for specific events are confirmed through official notices and local health facilities. Always check the latest information before travelling.

How to Participate

Communities and partners can support health events in the following ways.

  • Check with your nearest health facility for local campaign dates and times
  • Bring children for scheduled immunisation and growth monitoring
  • Share official information from MPHA and avoid spreading unverified messages
  • Support community health workers and volunteers during outreach activities
  • Contact MPHA for partnership or formal collaboration on approved events
