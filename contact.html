Contact Us

Get in touch with the Morobe Provincial Health Authority for general enquiries, feedback and official correspondence.

Use the contact details below for general, administrative and non-emergency enquiries. For medical emergencies or urgent clinical care, go to the nearest health facility or use the emergency contacts listed on this site.

Please note that response times for general email and written enquiries may vary. For facility-specific services (appointments, clinic times), contact the facility directly via the Facilities Directory.

🚨

Medical Emergency?

For life-threatening emergencies, go to the nearest hospital or health centre immediately, or call official emergency numbers. Do not rely solely on website contact forms for urgent care.
